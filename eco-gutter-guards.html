Why Eco Gutter Guards

Zero Maintenance

Stop climbing ladders every season. Once installed, debris stays out and water flows freely — no clogs, no call-outs, ever.

Protects Your Home

Blocked gutters cause roof damage, fascia rot, and foundation flooding. Eco Guards keep water where it belongs — away from your home.

Built for BC Rainfall

Engineered for the heavy rain and leaf fall common across the Lower Mainland and Fraser Valley — not a cheap import guard.

25-Year Warranty

Backed by a product warranty and our own workmanship guarantee. We stand behind every install the same way we do every roof.

How It's Done
Step 01

Free Site Quote

We assess your gutter type, roofline, and debris load — then give you a clear price with no surprises.

Step 02

Gutter Clean First

We always clean your gutters before installing guards. No point guarding a blocked gutter — included in every install.

Step 03

Guard Installation

Eco Guards are fitted, trimmed, and locked to your gutter profile. No screws into your fascia, no voided roof warranties.

Step 04

Flow Test & Sign-Off

We run water through the full system before we leave. You sign off on the job. Done.
